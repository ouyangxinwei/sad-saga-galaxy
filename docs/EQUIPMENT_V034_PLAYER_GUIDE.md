# V0.34 Player Guide

Equipped weapons, armor, and relics can now activate battle effects: bonus attacks, critical follow-ups, counters, shields, healing, and MP recovery. Triggers have chance, cooldown, and per-turn limits. Dead characters cannot trigger effects, and counters cannot chain recursively. Active skills still use MP and normal MP regeneration is unchanged. Activations appear in the battle log; detailed skip reasons are in Debug Logger.
