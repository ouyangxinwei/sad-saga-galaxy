# Character Detail V1.0 — Player Guide

## Open a character profile
1. On Home, tap any character in the five-member party panel.
2. On Formation, tap a character card in either the current party or the owned-card list.
3. The character detail dialog opens directly.

## What the profile shows
- Level, rarity, class, rebirth, and total power.
- Maximum HP and maximum MP.
- ATK, DEF, SPD, and critical rate.
- Currently equipped relic and its attribute bonuses.
- All active class skills, including target, MP cost, and effect values.
- The class passive ability.

## Change equipment
Use **Open equipment manager** in the profile to jump to Forge with that character preselected. Equip or remove relics there.

## Change your formation
On the Formation page:
1. Tap the small slot number below a formation position to select the position to edit.
2. Tap an owned character card to open its profile.
3. Choose **Place in selected slot** in the profile to assign it.

Tapping a card opens its details; it no longer directly assigns it.

## HP and MP note
The profile displays maximum HP and MP. Current HP and MP during a battle are managed by that battle and are visible on the battle screen; they are not saved as persistent out-of-battle character values.
