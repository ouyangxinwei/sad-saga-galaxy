# V0.36 Player Guide — Forge

1. Open Forge and select a current party member.
2. Choose Weapon, Armor, or Relic.
3. Browse the catalog; the count shows how many you own.
4. Select an item to inspect its stats and trigger details.
5. If owned, compare current stats with projected stats after equipping.
6. Choose Equip or Unequip. If another character wears the item, choose Locate wearer.

The same item cannot be equipped by two characters simultaneously. Items marked Not owned are catalog entries only. Relic crafting and the furnace remain available.
