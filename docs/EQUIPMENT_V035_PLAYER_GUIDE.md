# V0.35 Player Guide — Smarter Equipment Synergy

Automatic battles now take equipped special effects into account when choosing skills.

- Damage-focused equipment can make attacks and area attacks more attractive.
- Healing equipment can encourage healing when teammates are injured.
- Shield and counter equipment can make defensive and taunt skills more valuable.
- MP-restoring equipment can make affordable active skills more attractive.

Your equipment does not need to be a particular named item. The AI reads the effects configured on the equipment you actually wear.

Active skills still consume MP, normal MP regeneration is unchanged, and characters without relevant equipment keep the previous base scoring. No save reset or Forge change is required.
